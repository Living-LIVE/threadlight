# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

Threadlight welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds and perspectives. Be
respectful, specific, and constructive. Assume good intent, make room for correction, and focus
discussion on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, hateful or discriminatory language, threats, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, or sharing credentials and private
conversation content.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when this
standard is not met. Report concerning behavior privately to a project maintainer through GitHub;
do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.
